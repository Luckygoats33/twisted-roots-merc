Why a bakery inside a mercantile

Because a store nobody visits can't stock anything.

The honest business answer: food is what gets people through the door on a normal morning. Once they're here, they remember they're out of dog food, or they see the tarps and think about the wind coming Thursday.

The nicer answer: Siletz didn't have a place to sit down with a cinnamon roll and find out what everybody's up to. Now it does.
