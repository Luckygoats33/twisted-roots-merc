Finding the place

Look for the carved sign.

It's the one Eric made — Oregon-shaped, with the roots on the left and the wave on the right. You'll know it.

  • Parking — out front, gravel, room for trailers along the side.
  • The Yard — around back, pull straight in, we load.
  • Pickup holds — behind the front counter. Just say your name.
  • Accessible — ramp at the front door, single level inside, wide aisles.
  • Dogs — welcome, and there's a biscuit jar at the register for them.
