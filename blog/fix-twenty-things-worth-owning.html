Fix It

Twenty Things Worth Owning Before You Need Them

None of this is exciting. All of it is the difference between fixing something tonight and driving to Newport tomorrow.

3 minute read  ·  Twisted Roots Merc, Siletz

The whole reason this store exists is the forty minute round trip for a seven dollar part. The next best thing to a store two minutes away is a shelf in your own garage with the twenty things that come up over and over. This is that list, in the order we would buy it.

The five that come after

If the twenty above are covered and you want to keep going: a torpedo level, a hand saw, a pry bar, a drain snake, and a good pair of channel-lock adjustable pliers in a second size. That set handles nearly everything a house throws at a person short of a trade job.

For anybody with a chainsaw, add a file kit and a spare chain. For anybody with a trailer, add a hitch pin and a light adapter. For a house on a well, add whatever your pump needs and know which breaker it is on.

Buy the middle of the range

The cheapest hand tools fail at the worst moment and the most expensive ones are for people who use them daily. The middle is where the value is, particularly for wrenches, pliers and anything that has to hold torque. Buy tape measures and utility knives cheap, because you will lose them. Buy wrenches, pliers and levels once.

The exception is anything that protects you: eye protection, gloves, and a voltage tester. Buy those properly.

Where to put it

One place, not four. A single box or one shelf, and everything goes back to it. The most common reason people drive to town is not that they lack a tool, it is that they cannot find the one they have. Label the shelf if you have to. And keep the flashlight, the water shutoff information and the breaker panel key together somewhere everyone in the house knows about, because the water shutoff is the one piece of knowledge that has to be available to whoever is home.

All twenty of these are on the Fix It wall, and the storm-specific version of the list is over in storm supplies. If we are missing something you needed, tell us, and if two people ask it goes on the shelf permanently.
