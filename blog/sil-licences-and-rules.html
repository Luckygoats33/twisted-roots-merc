Around Siletz

Licences, Tags and Where to Check the Rules That Actually Apply

Why this website will never print a season date or a bag limit, and the short list of places that are worth trusting instead.

3 minute read  ·  Twisted Roots Merc, Siletz

People come in and ask us what the limit is. We do not answer that, and it is worth explaining why, because it is not us being unhelpful.

The rules move

Fishing and hunting regulations in Oregon are not a fixed thing you learn once. Seasons open and close in response to how a run is doing. Emergency regulations get issued mid-season. Whether you may keep a wild fish, whether a stretch of river is open, what gear is allowed, how many, how big — all of it can be different from one year to the next, and different between the mainstem, tidewater, the tributaries and the bay.

So a page on a small store's website that printed a number would be wrong eventually, and probably sooner than that. And the person carrying the consequence of a wrong number is you, standing on a gravel bar, not us.

Where to look

  • Oregon Department of Fish and Wildlife is the source. Sport fishing regulations, hunting regulations, licences, tags, the draw, harvest cards and in-season updates all live with them. The website is myodfw.com and it is the first stop for everything.
  • Recreational shellfish closures are a separate matter and are handled by Oregon Department of Agriculture. Biotoxin closures for razor clams, bay clams, mussels and sometimes crab viscera are announced there and by ODFW, and they are a health issue rather than a fishing issue. Check before every trip. Always.
  • Oregon State Police publishes current firearms rules if that is the question.
  • If you are on or near tribal land or fishing waters that the Confederated Tribes of Siletz Indians manage, that is a separate framework with its own rules. It is not ours to summarise. Find out where you are and ask the right office rather than guessing.

What you generally need in your pocket

Broadly speaking, an angler needs a current licence, and for salmon, steelhead, sturgeon and halibut a combined harvest card that gets marked when you keep a fish. Hunters need a licence plus the right tag, and some tags come out of a controlled draw with an application window months ahead of the season. Youth and senior rules differ. Some shellfish need a licence and some do not, and that changes too.

All of that is a sketch of the shape, not a statement of the current rules. Read the current synopsis. It is free, it is online, and it takes twenty minutes.

Buy the licence before the drive

You can buy licences and tags online and carry them on your phone, or print them. Doing that at the kitchen table is much better than discovering the problem at a boat ramp with no signal, because there are a lot of places between here and the water where your phone does nothing. See the dead zone problem.

Same goes for the draw. Controlled hunt applications have a deadline that is months before anybody is thinking about hunting, and missing it costs you the whole season. Put it in a calendar with a reminder, because nobody remembers it in the spring.

The rules that are not written down

Separate from regulation, there is the set of things that keep places open and keep people speaking to each other. Close gates. Do not block a ramp. Do not park on private ground. Pack out your line and your bait tubs. Give somebody who got there first their room. There is more of this in the access piece.

A warden checking your card is a minor inconvenience. A landowner locking a gate because of what somebody did last season is permanent.

What we will tell you

We will tell you what is running, what people are using, where the ramps are, which stretch of road is washed out, and whether the river is fishable. We will sell you line, hooks, bait, boots and a cooler off the outdoors shelf, and ammunition and field gear off the hunt wall.

What we will not do is give you a legal conclusion in a small town from behind a counter, because we would be guessing. Check ODFW. It takes less time than the drive.
