The Yard

Why the Racks Stop at Eight Feet

We are not a lumberyard and we are not going to act like one. Here is exactly what that means and who to call instead.

3 minute read  ·  Twisted Roots Merc, Siletz

People ask about this at the counter most weeks, usually somewhere between polite curiosity and mild frustration. So here is the plain answer.

What the Yard is

Eight to ten lumber SKUs. A handful of concrete and masonry products in a dry shed, up on pallets and out of the weather. Landscape gravel. Everything 8 feet or shorter. That is the whole inventory and it is not going to grow much.

It exists for one situation, which we will state as plainly as we can:

I am halfway through this and I need one more thing, today.

You are three-quarters through a fence and you are two pickets short. You pulled a deck board and the one underneath is soft too. You dug the post hole bigger than you meant to and now you need a second bag of fast-set. It is four in the afternoon on a Sunday and Newport is forty minutes each way.

What the Yard is not

It is not a lumberyard. Siletz River Lumber is the lumberyard, they are good at it, they carry the lengths, grades and sheet goods we do not, and they deliver. Toledo Do it Best carries the deep hardware, the block, the fencing panels and the twelve variants of a connector that we keep exactly one of.

We are not competing with either of them and it would be foolish to try. Two people cannot stock a lumberyard, and a store that pretends to have something it does not is worse than useless, because you drove over here on the strength of it.

Twelves and fourteens require a trailer or a rack, a longer rack building, and a delivery relationship we do not have. And the person who needs a twelve is usually building something big enough that they should be getting a proper load anyway.

What we do stock and why each one

  • 2x4 and 2x6, plain and treated. The two sizes that cover framing repairs, rails, beds and small builds.
  • 4x4x8 pressure treated, ground contact. The single most-asked-for item in the Yard. Fence posts, mailbox posts, gate posts.
  • 1x4 and 1x6 boards. Trim, fence boards, batten, shelving, compost bins.
  • Cedar pickets. Because one broken picket is the most common fence job there is and nobody should have to buy a bundle.
  • 2x4 foot project panels, plain and treated. A quarter sheet that fits in a car and patches almost anything.
  • Concrete, fast-set, sand mix, mortar, form tubes, gravel. Kept dry, because paper bags and coastal humidity do not get along.

Merchandised in kits, because that is how the job goes

A 4x4, a bag of fast-set and a handful of exterior screws sit on the same pallet. You do not have to walk three aisles and remember the fastener. That is a small thing and it is most of what we can offer that a bigger store cannot.

If we do not have it

We will tell you straight, and we will give you the number for whoever does. We will also write it down, because if the same thing gets asked for enough times it goes on the shelf permanently, for everybody, not just the person who asked. That is slower than special-ordering and it is the difference between a store and an errand service.

Check yard stock before you drive over, especially pressure treated after a wet week. Or come find us and pull the truck around back. We will help you load it.
