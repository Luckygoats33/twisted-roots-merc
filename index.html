Storm Ready

The power goes out here. It just does. Oregon's emergency folks say work toward being two weeks ready — we'd settle for you not driving to Newport in a windstorm for D batteries.

When a system is coming in, we flip the store into Storm Mode: reorder minimums jump, the wall gets restocked, and this website shows you exactly what's left.
