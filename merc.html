Being honest about it

What we don't carry

We're not a Home Depot and we're not pretending to be. Siletz River Lumber does lumber, Toledo Do it Best does the deep hardware selection, and they're both good at it.

You won't find power-tool ecosystems, appliances, full paint-color mixing, electrical panels, doors and windows, roofing pallets, cabinets, flooring, tile, or contractor-sized bulk here.

What you will find is the everyday 80% — deep enough that we actually have it — plus the food, the coffee and a yard out back with enough lumber and concrete to finish the job you started Saturday morning.
