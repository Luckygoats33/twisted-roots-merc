# Stop query-string duplicates of the stock search.
#
# ?q= is deliberately NOT blocked. It is the parameter shop.js reads,
# so it is the URL the sitelinks searchbox has to point at, and it is
# how the repair guides deep-link a pre-filtered shelf ("toilet
# flappers, on the shelf" → shop.html?q=flapper). Blocking it would
# close off both. Duplicate-content risk is handled by the canonical
# tag on shop.html instead, which is the right tool for it.
Disallow: /*?search=
