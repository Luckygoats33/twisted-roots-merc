How the basket works

Three steps, none of them a checkout

You fill a basket here. We pull it, put your name on it and set it behind the front counter. It waits there until we close. Nothing is charged on this site and no card details ever touch it, because the register is at the counter and always has been.

If something you want isn't here, say so. Carrie reads the requests on Sunday nights, and enough neighbours asking for the same thing is how half of Aisle 4 got there in the first place.
