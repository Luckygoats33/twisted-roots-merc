Why this wall exists

The power goes out here. It just does.

High wind, ice, and trees across the line — that's the Oregon Coast in winter. Oregon's emergency management folks recommend working toward being two weeks ready: water, food, light, batteries, first aid, blankets, a way to charge a phone and a radio that doesn't need the grid. We'd settle for you not driving to Newport at 8pm in a windstorm for D cells.
